Our story

A neighborhood table with a coastal heart.

Coastal Kitchen is a seafood restaurant at 8945 Forest Road in Santa Rosa, California, with a premium-casual dining room built around coastal-inspired cooking.

The idea is simple: thoughtfully prepared seafood, a relaxed room and a team that makes every guest feel welcome, whether it’s a quick weeknight dinner or a long celebration.
